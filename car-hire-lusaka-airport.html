Car Hire at Kenneth Kaunda International

Flying into Kenneth Kaunda International Airport (LUN)? Chaliko Car Hire Limited is based in Northmead, Lusaka, and arranges vehicle hire and airport transfers around your flight schedule — self-drive or with a professional driver.

Whether you need a sedan for business meetings in town, an SUV for a group, or a 4x4 ready for onward travel, our fully insured fleet is available for daily, weekly, and monthly hire, with pickup coordinated once you land.

How Airport Hire Works

01

Tell Us Your Flight Details

Book online, by phone, or via WhatsApp with your arrival date and time, and the vehicle you'd like.

02
