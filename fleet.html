What You Need

Hire Requirements

01

Valid Driver's Licence

A Zambian or internationally recognised driver's licence is required. The primary driver must be at least 23 years of age at the time of hire.

02

National ID or Passport

A valid National Registration Card (NRC) or passport is required for identity verification at vehicle pick-up. No exceptions.

03

Refundable Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit is required at pick-up. The amount varies by vehicle class and will be fully refunded upon safe return of the vehicle.
