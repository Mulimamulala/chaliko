Requirements

What You Need to Hire

A few simple requirements to get you on the road safely and legally.

Valid Driver's Licence

A valid Zambian or international driver's licence is required. Minimum age of 23 years for most vehicle classes.

National ID / Passport

A valid national identity card or passport for identity verification at the time of pick-up.

Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit is required at collection. The amount varies by vehicle class and duration.
