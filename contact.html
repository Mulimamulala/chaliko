FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

What documents do I need to hire a car?
You need a valid driver's licence (Zambian or international), a national ID or passport, and a completed booking form. A refundable security deposit is also required at pick-up.
Can I get a car delivered to my location or the airport?
Yes. We offer airport and hotel delivery in Lusaka. Delivery to other locations may attract a small surcharge. Please mention this when making your booking.
Is insurance included in the hire price?
Yes, all vehicles come with comprehensive third-party insurance. We also offer additional collision damage waiver (CDW) coverage for extra peace of mind.
Can I hire a car with a driver?
Absolutely. We offer a chauffeur option for an additional K50 per day. Our drivers are experienced, professional, and familiar with routes across Zambia.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations made at least 48 hours before the scheduled pick-up are free of charge. Cancellations within 48 hours may incur a 20% fee. No-shows are charged in full.
