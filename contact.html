Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a credit card to book?

No. We accept mobile money (MTN/Airtel), bank transfer, cash at pick-up, and debit/credit cards. A 30% deposit confirms your reservation — the balance is payable at collection.

What is the minimum rental period?

The minimum rental period is one day (24 hours). We also offer discounted weekly and monthly rates for longer hires — contact us for a tailored quote.

Can I drive to other countries?

Cross-border travel is permitted but requires prior approval from Chaliko Car Hire Limited and an additional cross-border fee. Please inform us at the time of booking so we can arrange the necessary documentation.

What documents do I need to hire a vehicle?

You will need a valid driver's licence (Zambian or international; minimum age 23), a national ID or passport, and the funds for the security deposit. All documents are verified at collection.

Is fuel included in the rental price?

No. All vehicles are provided with a full tank and must be returned full. A fuel surcharge option is available at collection if you prefer us to handle refuelling on return.

Can I add a professional driver?

Yes. We can arrange a vetted, professional driver for an additional K50 per day. Advance notice is appreciated so we can assign the right person for your journey.
