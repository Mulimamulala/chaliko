Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Service

Chaliko Car Hire Limited was founded with one clear goal: to make reliable and affordable vehicle rental accessible to every Zambian and visitor to our beautiful country. Over the years, we have grown from a humble fleet into one of Zambia's most trusted car hire companies, serving clients across six major cities and beyond.

We serve individuals, families, corporate clients, NGOs, and tourists. We deliver tailored solutions for every need, whether it is a quick city commute or a cross-country safari adventure. Our team is dedicated to ensuring that every client drives away with confidence and peace of mind.

From our base in Lusaka, we operate across Ndola, Kitwe, Livingstone, Kabwe, and Chipata. Wherever your journey takes you in Zambia, Chaliko is with you every kilometer of the way.
