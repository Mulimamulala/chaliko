Our Story

Built on Trust,
Driven by Service

Chaliko Car Hire Limited was founded with one clear goal: to make reliable and affordable vehicle rental accessible to every Zambian and visitor to our beautiful country.

Over the years, we have grown from a small fleet into one of Zambia's most trusted car hire companies, serving clients across Lusaka, Ndola, Kitwe, Livingstone, and beyond.

We serve individuals, families, corporate clients, NGOs, and tourists — providing tailored solutions for every need, whether a quick city run or a cross-country safari adventure.
